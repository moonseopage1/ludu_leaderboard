📊 Ranking Formula

Score = Avg × LossFactor × MatchFactor

① Avg — Average Points per Match

Avg = Total Points ÷ Matches Played

Measures how well you score each game. A player with 24 pts in 8 matches has Avg = 3.00.

② LossFactor — Loss Penalty

LossFactor = 1 − (Last-place Finishes ÷ Matches)

Reduces your score if you often finish 4th. Never lost → 1.0 (no penalty). Lost half your games → 0.5.

③ MatchFactor — Experience Multiplier

MatchFactor = min(1, Matches ÷ 10)

Scales score down for players with fewer than 10 games. At 10+ games it becomes 1.0 (full score). Prevents a newcomer with 1 lucky win from topping the table.

📌 Example

Player: 8 matches, 22 pts, 2 last-place finishes

Avg       = 22 ÷ 8 = 2.75
LossFactor = 1 − (2 ÷ 8) = 0.75
MatchFactor = 8 ÷ 10 = 0.80
Score = 2.75 × 0.75 × 0.80 = 1.65
